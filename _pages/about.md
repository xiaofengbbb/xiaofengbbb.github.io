Hi! I’m **Yan Feng (冯岩)**, a **Ph.D. candidate (Year 2)** at **Chang’an University**, affiliated with the **School of Data Science & Artificial Intelligence**.

My research focuses on **AI security for recommender systems**, with an emphasis on **poisoning attack detection**, **anomaly forensics**, and **reliability and stability-aware security mechanisms**. Recently, I have also extended these security studies to **traffic route recommendation systems**, aiming to build **controllable, stability-aware, and explainable** defenses in closed-loop transportation scenarios.

## Research Interests
- **Recommender System Security**
- **Robust / Explainable Detection**
- **Stability-aware Security**
- **Traffic Route Recommendation (Application)**

## Publications (Selected)
- **IEEE TDSC (CCF-A, SCI-Q1)**: *Is There A Bottom Line for Poisoning? Detecting High-Concealed Injection Attacks for Recommendation*  
- **IEEE TKDE (CCF-A, SCI-Q1, Survey)**: *Attacks and Detections in Recommender Systems: A Comprehensive Analysis for Models, Progresses, and Trends*  
- **PAKDD 2026 (CCF-C, Oral, Student Travel Award)**: *Are There Any Hidden Agents in Your Recommendations? Anomaly Detection via Structure Purification and Stability Verification*  

## Co-author
- **IEEE TDSC (CCF-A, SCI-Q1)**: *Meet Trick With Trick: Revealing Collusion Intentions in Highly Concealed Poisoning Behavior*
- **Elsevier ESWA (CCF-C, SCI-Q1)**: *Shoot The Arrow at The Target: Personalized Adversarial Defense Driven by Dynamic Rewards*  

## Visiting Plan (CSC)
